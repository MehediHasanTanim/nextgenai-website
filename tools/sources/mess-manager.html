The challenge

Shared living creates a surprisingly complex monthly হিসাব.

Meal counts change daily. Different members buy groceries, pay bills, deposit money and host guests. At month end, one manager has to turn all of it into a fair, explainable settlement.

Before Mess Manager

✕ Meal counts scattered across notebooks, chats or spreadsheets.
✕ Manual meal-rate calculations are easy to get wrong.
✕ Member-paid বাজার and deposits are hard to reconcile.
✕ Month-end হিসাব can become an argument rather than a transparent calculation.

The product idea

✓One manager, one phone, one source of truth.
✓Fast meal entry and automatic meal-rate calculation.
✓Every balance traces back to source transactions.
✓Core workflows remain fully usable without internet.

Product system

Everything needed to run a mess—without turning it into accounting software.

The product is organized around the manager's recurring jobs rather than accounting terminology.

🍚

Daily Meals

Enter every member from one fast sheet. Use breakfast/lunch/dinner or total units, including half meals.

🛒

বাজার & Expenses

Record grocery and shared expenses, who paid, and whether the expense contributes to meal rate.

৳

Deposits

Track cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, bank and other deposits with member-level history.

🧾

Utility Bills

Allocate electricity, gas, water and Wi-Fi equally, selectively, or through custom amounts.

⚖

Settlement

Explain every payable and credit, reconcile totals, freeze the final rate and safely close the month.

↻

Backup & Recovery

Create local backups, validate before restore, and protect current data with a safety backup.

Designed around the rhythm of a real mess.

UX direction

Financial clarity without financial jargon.

Instead of ambiguous positive and negative values, the interface states exactly what a balance means: “Mess owes Rahim” or “Karim owes mess.”

✓Thumb-friendly controls for daily entry.
✓বাংলা and English are first-class interfaces.
✓Closed months are protected from accidental editing.
✓Color supports meaning but never carries it alone.

Technical design

Local-first by architecture, not as a fallback.

SQLite is the source of truth. Financial logic is deterministic and testable outside the UI. Internet access is optional rather than required.

PresentationFlutter UI, Riverpod state, routing, localization and design system.
ApplicationUse cases coordinating meals, expenses, settlement, reports and backups.
DomainPure-Dart Money, MealUnits, allocation, meal-rate, balance and settlement engines.
DataDrift/SQLite, transactions, migrations, files, secure storage and notifications.

Product outcome

A small utility with a very concrete job.

Mess Manager replaces the notebook + calculator + chat-message workflow with a trustworthy local system that remains understandable to the people whose money is being managed.

✓ Core workflows operate without internet
✓ Every settlement amount is explainable
✓ Month-end needs no external spreadsheet
