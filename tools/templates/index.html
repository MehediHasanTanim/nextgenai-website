How we build

Four principles behind every product

01

Bangla first

Not translated afterwards. Language, number formats, local payment methods and everyday phrasing are designed in from day one.

02

Offline first

Connectivity in Bangladesh varies. Our apps work where you are, and sync when they can — the phone is the source of truth.

03

Private by design

Health and money are personal. Local encrypted storage, no mandatory accounts, and only encrypted backups ever leave the device.

04

AI that assists

AI drafts, explains and answers from your own data. A person confirms before anything is saved, prescribed or decided.
