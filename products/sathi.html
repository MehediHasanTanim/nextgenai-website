Overview

What Sathi is

Sathi (সাথী) is a Bangla-first, voice-first assistant designed for elderly Bangladeshi users who find typing and complicated menus hard. Instead of learning an interface, they just talk: “আমার ছেলেকে ফোন করে দাও”, “কাল সকাল ৮টায় ওষুধ খাওয়ার কথা মনে করিয়ে দিও”.

It is a practical task assistant rather than a chatbot. Sathi confirms what it understood before acting, answers out loud, and keeps large, simple screens as a fallback. Adult children can set it up for their parents.

Features

What it does

01

Talk naturally in Bangla

Understands everyday Bangladeshi Bangla, with spoken replies so there is nothing to read.

02

Reminders that work offline

Medicine and appointment reminders by voice — create, snooze, repeat, list today’s.

03

Calls and messages

“ছেলেকে ফোন করো” — call or message family by saying their name.

04

Notes and voice notes

Speak a note; hear it back later.

05

Time, date and weather

Simple answers, spoken clearly.

06

Emergency assistance

One large button to alert family contacts.

07

Confirm before acting

Sathi repeats what it understood and waits for “হ্যাঁ” before it saves or calls.

08

Private and local-first

Personal and health information stays on the device wherever possible.

Approach

How it's built

01

Voice first, screen second

Big text, big buttons and a single clear action per screen.

02

Not a diagnosis tool

It helps with daily tasks; it never gives medical diagnosis or treatment advice.

03

Local-first

Reminders, notes and contacts live on the phone, with optional backup.
