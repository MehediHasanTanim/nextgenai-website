Overview

What Chamber is

Chamber (চিকিৎসা সাথী) is a Bangladesh-focused platform that lets a doctor run the whole chamber from one place — patients, appointments, the daily queue, consultations, prescriptions, reports, payments and staff — replacing paper prescriptions, notebooks, spreadsheets and chat threads.

The workflow follows how a chamber really runs: staff manage operations, an assistant doctor supports clinical work, the doctor makes clinical decisions — and AI assists the doctor. It is designed for Bangladesh’s variable connectivity, in Bangla and English, on mobile and web.

Features

What it does

01

Appointments & live queue

Schedule visits, check patients in, call the next one, skip or recall — with a display mode for the waiting room.

02

Patient records

Longitudinal history, vitals, allergies, diagnoses and reports in one profile.

03

Fast prescriptions

Medicine search, favourites and templates, then print, PDF, WhatsApp or SMS to the patient.

04

AI patient summary

A concise, source-linked summary of a patient’s history before the consultation starts.

05

AI prescription drafting

Draft a prescription from the diagnosis — with drug-interaction and allergy checks — for the doctor to review.

06

Reports & investigations

Order, receive and interpret lab and imaging reports.

07

Payments & analytics

Collect fees, track revenue across chambers, and see practice trends.

08

Roles & permissions

Doctor, assistant doctor, reception and admin — each with the access they need and an audit trail.
