Overview

What ShohojSathi is

ShohojSathi (সহজসাথী) is built for families in Bangladesh. It learns a child’s needs, interests and comfort preferences, then organises small, practical activities that fit naturally into daily life — so learning feels like part of a familiar day, not pressure.

One app holds two calm spaces. Parent Mode is PIN-protected and gives caregivers full control of profiles, goals, progress, observations, safety and data. Child Mode offers large visual cards, predictable navigation and sound only after interaction, with no text-heavy header to distract.

Features

What it does

01

Today’s small plan

Daily activities matched to the child’s active goals, interests and comfort.

02

Guided activities

Preparation, one step at a time, pause support and simple outcome notes.

03

Communication board

Familiar request cards with pictures and local Bangla audio.

04

Routines & First–Then

Visual steps, a timer and transitions that make change more predictable.

05

Progress & observations

Weekly summaries, skill history and notes on positive or difficult moments.

06

Caregiver learning & safety

Micro-lessons, an emergency information card and professionally reviewed safety guidance.

07

Parent Mode & Child Mode

Planning and sensitive information stay with the caregiver; the child gets simple, approved tools.

08

Understand the child first

Captures communication, interests, sensory comfort and independence to suggest relevant starting goals.

Approach

How it's built

01

Trust by design

No mandatory account. Profiles, history, routines and notes stay encrypted on the device; backup and export are always the caregiver’s decision.

02

Clear boundaries

ShohojSathi does not diagnose and is not a substitute for therapy or emergency care.

03

Human-reviewed content

Bangla content, activities and safety messages receive language and professional review.

ShohojSathi does not diagnose any condition and is not a substitute for therapy, professional advice or emergency medical care.
