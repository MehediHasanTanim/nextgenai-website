Overview

What Hisab is

Hisab (হিসাব) is a personal-finance app built for how people in Bangladesh actually handle money: a little cash, a bank account, a credit card, bKash and Nagad — all at once. Every account sits side by side, so one screen answers “where is my money, and where did it go?”

All financial data lives in a local encrypted database on the phone. There is no account to create and no server holding your numbers. Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox are used only for optional, encrypted backup and restore — so a lost phone never means lost records.

Features

What it does

01

Every account in one view

Cash, bank, mobile wallets and credit cards, each as a clean monogram tile — no borrowed bank logos.

02

Budgets that warn you early

Set category limits and see live progress, with gentle alerts before you overspend.

03

Savings goals

Track targets like an emergency fund or Umrah, with monthly contribution plans and a projected finish date.

04

Recurring & cash-flow calendar

Salary, rent and EMIs on a calendar, with a triage flow for recurring items that were missed.

05

Loans, cards & investments

Keep money you owe, money owed to you, card dues and investments alongside daily spending.

06

Capture from SMS and receipts

On Android, opt in to turn bKash, Nagad, Rocket and bank SMS into transactions — parsed on the phone, never uploaded. Receipt photos (OCR) save typing too.

07

Reports and Ask Hisab

Income vs. expense trends and “where your money went” — plus an opt-in AI assistant (bring your own key) that answers questions about your own data and adds entries from plain text.

08

Backup you control

Encrypted backup to your own cloud with a backup password, restore, and conflict-aware sync.

Approach

How it's built

01

Local-first

The phone is the source of truth. The app works fully offline and never needs a server of ours.

02

Bilingual by design

Every label has a Bangla and an English line. Bangla is the default; English is one tap away.

03

Quiet privacy

App lock with PIN or fingerprint, encrypted storage, and AI only when you switch it on.
