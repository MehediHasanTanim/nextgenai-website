Overview

What Hisab Dokan is

Hisab Dokan (দোকানের হিসাব) is business management for the small shopkeeper — the grocery, pharmacy, hardware or cloth shop that still runs on a notebook of বাকি (credit) entries. It replaces the notebook, not the way of working: record a sale, note what was paid and what is still due, update stock, and see the customer’s balance — in under a minute.

On top sits a Bangla-first AI assistant. Type “Rahim ১৫০০ টাকার মাল নিয়েছে, ৫০০ টাকা দিয়েছে” and Hisab Dokan drafts the entry — customer, amount, paid and due — then asks you to confirm before anything is saved.

Features

What it does

01

Customer & supplier ledgers

A running বাকি হিসাব for every customer and supplier, with who owes what at a glance.

02

Cash & credit sales, purchases

Fast sale and purchase entry with returns, receipts and PDF invoices to share.

03

Products & inventory

Stock levels, low-stock alerts and the value of stock on hand — import a catalogue from a spreadsheet.

04

All the money accounts

Cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket and bank balances, with transfers between them.

05

Due reminders

Polite, pre-written reminders to customers over WhatsApp or SMS, drafted for you.

06

Daily summary & reports

Sales, expenses, profit, dues and inventory — daily, weekly and monthly.

07

Ask Hisab

Ask questions about your own data: “Which product sold most this month?”, “Who owes me the most?”

08

Natural-language entry

Describe a transaction in plain Bangla; review the draft; confirm.

Approach

How it's built

01

Offline-first

Record transactions with no connection; sync and back up when you’re online.

02

AI that asks first

The assistant drafts, you confirm. It answers only from your own business data.

03

Bangla, then English

Bangla numerals, local payment methods and everyday shop vocabulary throughout.
