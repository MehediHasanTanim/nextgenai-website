Overview

What Jotno is

Jotno (যত্ন) is a private, offline-first family health manager for households in Bangladesh. Add each family member and keep their medications, prescriptions, doctors, appointments, vaccinations, lab reports and measurements together — instead of scattered across paper files, photos and chat threads.

The phone is the source of truth. There is no application backend and no mandatory account. Cloud providers are optional and only ever receive encrypted backups, so health information stays in the family’s hands. “আপনার পরিবারের স্বাস্থ্য তথ্য, আপনার কাছেই।”

Features

What it does

01

Family profiles

A dashboard for every member — age, blood group, allergies and what needs attention today.

02

Medications & reminders

Schedules, dose history and refill reminders so nobody misses a dose.

03

Prescriptions & documents

Keep prescriptions, reports and scans organised and searchable.

04

Health timeline

Every event — doses taken, readings, visits, reports — in one chronological view per person or family.

05

Measurements & charts

Blood pressure, blood sugar, weight and BMI, charted over time.

06

Vaccination schedules

Track doses given and what is due next, especially for children.

07

Emergency health card

Blood group, allergies, conditions, medicines and contacts — ready at a glance when it matters.

08

Encrypted backup & transfer

Back up to your own Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox; move to a new phone with a recovery phrase.

Approach

How it's built

01

Private by default

No sign-up and an optional app lock. Only encrypted backups leave the device; the app is free, and health data never reaches the ad SDK.

02

Bangla by default

Localised for Bangladesh from the start, with Bangla as the default language.

03

Honest scope

A record-keeper, not a doctor: it never gives medical advice and says so plainly.
