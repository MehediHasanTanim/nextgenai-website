Overview

What AI Tutor Bangladesh is

AI Tutor Bangladesh is a study companion for Class 9–10 Science students, built Bangla-first. Instead of answering from the open internet, it retrieves the relevant passage from the NCTB curriculum and explains it — in simple Bangla — with the chapter and page it came from.

Students can ask in text or with a photo of a question, get step-by-step help, then check understanding with quizzes. Progress and weak topics feed back into what the app suggests next, so practice goes where it is needed.

Features

What it does

01

Curriculum-grounded answers

Retrieval over NCTB content, with the source chapter and page shown for every explanation.

02

Explain, simplify, exemplify

Quick actions: explain simpler, give an example, show the formula, quiz me.

03

Ask with a photo

Snap a textbook question and choose: solve step by step, explain the concept, or give a hint.

04

Learn by chapter

Subject → chapter → topic, with summaries, key concepts, formulas and worked examples.

05

Quizzes

MCQ, true/false, short and numerical questions with timers and instant or end-of-test results.

06

Progress & weak topics

Study time, accuracy, streaks and topic mastery — with recommended practice.

07

Academic-integrity guardrails

Built to teach, not to hand over answers to copy.

08

Low-data friendly

Designed for students on limited connections.
